# Social Sentiment Scanner Playbook

Gauges crowd psychology, sentiment polarity, and retail narrative themes across active tickers.

## Outputs:
1. **Fear & Greed Index (0 - 100)**: From Extreme Fear to Extreme Greed.
2. **Prevailing Narrative Summary**: Core themes, retail biases, and sentiment divergence from fundamentals.
3. **Sentiment Divergence Flags**: Highlights where crowd panic creates oversold opportunities or euphoria creates exit risk.
